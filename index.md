# Tilework

Tilework is a Discord Activity of daily puzzles (Sudoku, Minesweeper, Cross Math and
Nonograms) that you play together in a voice channel. Everyone gets the same daily
puzzles, with a leaderboard for each, plus as many anytime puzzles as you like. Solving
earns Sparks, and servers can set up their own currency too.

Tilework is made by c³d and is currently in **alpha**.
